Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to essential questions regarding earnings, schedule, and training.

Q

Is there a base pay or hourly rate?

No, this is a 100% commission-based role. There is no fixed base pay. Your earnings are a percentage of the revenue you generate through chats, mails, and gifts. Your cut ranges from 35% to 50% depending on your total monthly revenue.

Q

When is payday? Is it weekly?

Payouts are monthly and are distributed on or before the 25th of the following month. We pay via GCash, PayMaya, or BPI.

Q

Is the training paid?

No, the training is unpaid. However, it is completely free to attend you will not be asked to pay anything. It is a 6-day intensive program to prepare you for the role.

Q

Do I automatically pass training?

No. On the 5th day, you will receive a decision on whether you pass and will be joining the Core Team. If you pass, the 6th day is your final training day, and you must sign a contract before starting paid work.
